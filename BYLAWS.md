## Buy-Ins & Payouts

### Buy-In
- Buy-in: $150 per person ($1,800 total pot)
- Payment due by draft day: August 23rd, 2026
- Payment method: Venmo @patisdoingstuff5

### Payout Structure
- 1st Place: $930
- 2nd Place: $300
- Season High Score: $60
- Weekly High Score: $25/week × 17 weeks = $425
- Survivor Pot: $85 (last team standing wins)
- Season High Score, Weekly High Score & Survivor Pot will all be paid out at the end of the year with the championship pay-out.
- Total: $1,800

### Survivor Pot Rules
- The lowest scoring team each week is eliminated from the survivor pot
- Last team standing wins the full $85 pot
- If two or more teams tie for the lowest score, all of the tied teams are eliminated
- If every remaining team ties for the lowest score, nobody is eliminated and the $85 pot is split evenly among them
- Eliminated teams still compete normally for all other payouts

### Payout Timeline
Payouts will be sent within 3 days of the final Monday Night game of the season.

## Draft

### Startup Draft
- Date: Sunday, August 23rd, 2026 @ 2:00 PM EDT
- Format: Snake draft with third round reversal
- Draft order: Randomized
- Available players: All players (veterans + rookies)
- This is a one-time startup draft: all future drafts are rookie-only
- No trading allowed during the startup draft. Trading during the rookie-only draft is allowed and encouraged.

### Startup Draft Format
- Rounds 1–4: Live draft @ 30 minutes per pick
- Rounds 5–29: Slow draft @ 8 hours per pick
- The switch to the slow draft portion is flexible. If the league decides on draft day to do more than 4 rounds @ 30 min per pick, we will decide that live in Sleeper.
- Please pick within 1 hour when possible during the slow draft portion
- Auto-pause nightly: 11 PM – 7 AM EDT

### Third Round Reversal
In a standard snake draft the pick order flips every round. Third round reversal modifies round 3 only:

- Round 1: Pick 1 → 12
- Round 2: Pick 12 → 1
- Round 3: Pick 12 → 1 (reversal same direction as round 2)
- Round 4+: Back to normal snake

This balances the draft by giving the pick 1 owner a back-to-back at the turn of rounds 2 and 3, matching the back-to-back the pick 12 owner gets at rounds 1 and 2 in a standard snake.

### Rookie Supplemental Drafts
- 5 rounds, linear order (non-snaking)
- Playoff teams seeded in order of playoff results. (League winner gets 1.12 for example)
- Non-playoff teams are sorted in order of regular season W-L record. Ties goes in favor of the team with less Max Points For (The team with the lower point total for gets the higher draft pick)
- Compensatory pick 1.13 awarded to the team with the highest start/sit % for the season (see Compensatory Picks)

| Picks | Who | Order |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| 1.01-1.06 | Non playoff teams | Worst record first; ties broken by max PF (lower max PF picks first) |
| 1.07-1.08 | Lost in round 1 (Week 15) | Worst record first, then max pf (lower max PF picks first) |
| 1.09-1.10 | Lost in the semifinal (Week 16) | Worst record first, then max pf (lower max PF picks first) |
| 1.11 | Lost in the championship | |
| 1.12 | Champion | |
| 1.13 | Start/sit % winner | See compensatory picks below |

### Compensatory Picks
Each year, pick 1.13 (end of round 1) is awarded to the team with the highest start/sit percentage for the season.

- Start/sit % = PF ÷ Max PF
- PF = actual points scored
- Max PF = maximum possible points if the perfect lineup had been set every week
- Trackable on the matchup tab each week in Sleeper or on the [League Website](https://dynasty-dashboard-ijio.onrender.com/)
- Commissioner manually awards the pick before the rookie draft

## Roster

### Roster Construction
- 1 QB, 2 RB, 3 WR, 1 TE, 3 FLEX (W/R/T), 15 Bench
- No Kicker, no Defense
- Taxi Squad: 4 spots (rookies only)
- Total roster spots: 23 active + 4 taxi = 27
- Bench/Roster spots may change in future seasons by league vote

### Taxi Squad
- Taxi squad is separate from your bench. Taxi players cannot play unless promoted to the active roster
- 4 spots, rookies only
- Must be filled before the start of the regular season (Sept 6th)
- Rookies may be moved from the taxi to the bench/starting lineup throughout the season but once moved cannot be moved back.
- Promoting a player off the taxi squad requires dropping someone from your active roster
- Rookies only for Taxi squad. Managers will be forced to add the player(s) to their active roster or release them to waivers.

### Injured Reserve
1 IR slot.

## Player Acquisitions

### FAAB Waivers
- Each team has a $200 FAAB budget for the season
- FAAB bidding is required every day.
- Waivers process daily at 7 AM EDT
- Weekly waivers clear Wednesday
- Players stay on waivers for 1 day after being dropped
- If top bids are tied, it reverts to traditional waiver wire priority

---

## Trades
- All trades process immediately. No review period.
- Draft pick trading is allowed (not in the startup draft)
- Week 14 trade deadline. Deals can be made up until playoffs begin
- No trading during the startup draft
- If a trade is suspected of collusion, any league member may flag it to the commissioner
- The Commissioner(s) reserves the right to reverse trades that threaten competitive integrity.

### Accidental Roster Moves
- Notify the commissioner immediately if a trade was sent or accepted in error
- Trades may only be voided due to legitimate error at commissioner's discretion
- For accidental drops, notify the commissioner before waivers. Once waivers run the transaction will not be reversed

---

## Scoring
- Half PPR (0.5 points per reception)
- No kicker scoring
- No defense scoring
- All other scoring settings are Sleeper default.
- Any scoring changes must be voted on by the league (75% participation, 55% in favor to pass)

---

## Tiebreaker Procedures
- Weekly High Score ties: the tied team with the higher start/sit % that week wins the $25
- Season High Score ties: the $60 is split evenly among the tied teams
- Survivor Pot ties: see Survivor Pot Rules
- Regular season ties: broken by total points scored on the season
- Playoff ties: broken by total points scored on the season

## Team Management

### Activity Rules
Owners are expected to be active year-round. Minimum activity includes:

- Set an active lineup every week
- Respond to trade offers in a timely manner
- Vote on the majority of rule change proposals

### Team Abandonment
- If an owner publicly declares they are quitting, the team is immediately classified as an orphan and the commissioner assumes control.
- Under the commissioners control, the only roster moves that are allowed are to just set the starting lineup every week.

### Orphan Teams
- Commissioner assumes control of orphan teams until a replacement is found
- Orphan teams may receive compensatory picks or reduced buy-in at commissioner's discretion if the roster is in poor shape

---

## Fair Play & Conduct

### Collusion
- Any owner who suspects collusion may flag it to the commissioner or in the league chat
- Commissioner reserves the right to pause or reverse transactions under a collusion review
- If collusion is confirmed, both teams are removed from playoff contention and are eligible to be removed from the league.

### Tanking
- Intentionally benching obvious starters to improve draft position is prohibited.
- Trading away players to rebuild is not tanking. That is dynasty strategy.

### Roster Dumping
- Dropping obviously valuable players without good reason is subject to commissioner sanctions
- Dropping borderline players is understood and acceptable

## Rule Changes
- Major rule changes require 75% league participation and 55% in favor to pass
- A follow-up vote will determine when approved changes take effect.
- Commissioner may make minor rule adjustments in the best interest of the league. Significant pushback will result in a revert

## Developer Notes (for dashboard build)

Not part of the Google Doc. These are the rules as the dashboard code
implements them, including a few decisions that aren't spelled out in
the bylaws text above.

- **Playoffs:** 6 teams, Weeks 15 to 17 (implied by the rookie draft table: 1.01-1.06 are the non-playoff teams)
- **Standings order** (`get_standings()`): all 12 teams by wins, then PF. Then the bottom 6 are re-sorted by wins, then Max PF, so lower Max PF ranks lower and picks earlier in the rookie draft
- **Rookie draft:** the 1-12 order repeats every round (linear). The compensatory pick exists in round 1 only
- **Survivor pot tie rule:** if two or more teams tie for lowest score in a week, ALL tied teams are eliminated
- **Survivor pot ends:** after Week 14 (regular season ends before playoffs in Week 15)
- **Trade deadline:** Week 14
- **Start/sit %:** PF ÷ Max PF, using Sleeper's season-to-date totals on each roster
- **Weekly high score:** single highest scorer each week wins $25
- **Weekly high score ties:** higher start/sit % that week wins (decided 2026-11-07). NOT built into the dashboard yet: it still shows every tied team. Needs a per-week start/sit %, but Sleeper only gives season-to-date PF/Max PF, so it would come from the difference between two weekly `start_sit` snapshots
- **Season high score ties:** $60 split evenly (decided 2026-11-07). The dashboard already shows "(tied)"
- **Payouts total:** $930 + $300 + $60 + $425 + $85 = $1,800. 1st place was $870 until 2026-11-07, when the old payouts only added up to $1,740 and the unassigned $60 went to 1st place
- **Season high score:** highest single-week score by any team across the season
- **FAAB:** each team starts with $200, remaining budget tracked via the transactions endpoint
- **IR slots:** 1
- **Waivers:** process daily at 7 AM EDT, players stay on waivers 1 day after being dropped
- **Bench lock:** players can't be dropped after their game starts (Sleeper enforces this automatically)
