## Draft

### Startup Draft
- Date: Sunday, August 23rd, 2026 @ 2:00 PM EDT
- Format: Snake draft with third round reversal
- Draft order: Randomized
- Available players: All players (veterans + rookies)
- This is a one-time startup draft — all future drafts are rookie-only
- No trading allowed during the startup draft
- CPU autopick is enabled — be on time and stay active

### Draft Format
- Rounds 1–4: Live @ 30 min/pick — be on the app at 2 PM
- Rounds 5–29: Slow draft @ 8 hours/pick
- Please pick within 1 hour when possible during slow draft
- Auto-pause nightly: 11 PM – 7 AM EDT

### Third Round Reversal
In a standard snake draft:
- Round 1: Pick 1 → 12
- Round 2: Pick 12 → 1
- Round 3: Pick 1 → 12 (standard)

With third round reversal:
- Round 1: Pick 1 → 12
- Round 2: Pick 12 → 1
- Round 3: Pick 12 → 1 (reversal — same direction as round 2)
- Round 4+: Back to normal snake

**Why:** Gives the pick 1 owner a back-to-back at the turn of rounds 2 and 3, balancing the back-to-back the pick 12 owner gets at rounds 1 and 2. Only affects round 3 — everything after is normal.

### Rookie Supplemental Drafts
- 5 rounds, linear order (non-snaking)
- Draft order determined by previous year's standings (worst record picks first)
- Held each August
- Compensatory pick 1.13 awarded to team with highest start/sit %

### Compensatory Picks
Each year, pick 1.13 (end of round 1) is awarded to the team with the highest start/sit % for the season.

- **Formula: Start/Sit % = PF ÷ Max PF**
- PF = actual points scored across the season
- Max PF = maximum possible points if the perfect lineup had been set every week
- Trackable on the matchup tab in Sleeper each week
- Commissioner manually awards the pick before the rookie draft
- Rewards active roster management — not just roster talent

## Roster

### Roster Construction
- 1 QB, 2 RB, 3 WR, 1 TE, 3 FLEX (W/R/T), 15 Bench
- No Kicker, no Defense
- Taxi Squad: 4 spots (rookies only)
- Total: 23 active + 4 taxi = 27 spots
- Bench spots may expand in future seasons by league vote

### Taxi Squad
- 4 spots, rookies only
- Must be filled before start of regular season (Sept 6th)
- Taxi squad does not count toward active roster
- Rookies may move freely between taxi and bench throughout the season
- Promoting a player off taxi requires dropping someone from active roster
- **Taxi eligibility lasts 1 year only** — Sleeper forces promote or drop after that

### Injured Reserve
- 2 IR slots
- Players designated as Out or IR are eligible to be placed on IR
- Placing a player on IR does not free up a roster spot — you must have room on your bench first

### Bench Lock Rule
- Bench players cannot be dropped after their game has started
- Plan ahead — once kickoff happens that player is locked for the week

## Player Acquisitions

### FAAB Waivers
- $200 FAAB budget per season per team
- FAAB bidding required every day — no free grabs, ever
- Waivers process daily at 7 AM EDT
- Weekly waivers clear Wednesday at 3 AM EDT
- Monday and Tuesday: players dropped after games stay on waivers until Wednesday 3 AM
- Players stay on waivers for 1 day after being dropped
- If top bids are tied, reverts to traditional waiver wire priority

## Trades
- All trades process immediately — no review period
- Draft pick trading is allowed
- **Trade deadline: Week 14 — no trades after Week 14**
- No trading during the startup draft
- If a trade is suspected of collusion, any league member may flag it to the commissioner
- Commissioner reserves the right to reverse trades that threaten competitive integrity
- Trades involving a player injured after the trade was sent: teams have 24 hours to flag with screenshots

### Accidental Roster Moves
- Notify commissioner immediately if a trade was sent or accepted in error
- Trades may only be voided due to legitimate error at commissioner's discretion
- For accidental drops: notify commissioner before waivers process — once waivers run the transaction will not be reversed

## Team Management

### Activity Rules
Owners are expected to be active year-round. Minimum activity:
- Set an active lineup every week
- Check chat and trade offers at least once per week during regular season
- Check chat and trade offers at least once per month during offseason
- Log in at least once per day during any draft
- Respond to trade offers in a timely manner
- Vote on the majority of rule change proposals

### Team Abandonment
- If an owner publicly declares they are quitting, the team is immediately an orphan and commissioner assumes control
- Quitting declarations are permanent — quitting owners are barred from reinstatement
- Owners wanting to leave in good standing must notify the commissioner first
- Owners who leave in good standing may be considered for reinstatement after one full season

### Orphan Teams
- Commissioner assumes control until a replacement is found
- League members have 3 days to suggest replacements
- Poll held for 1 day to vote on replacement
- Orphan teams may receive compensatory picks or reduced buy-in at commissioner's discretion

## Fair Play & Conduct

### Owner Conduct
- Trash talking is encouraged — keep it about football
- Personal attacks are not welcome and subject to discipline

### Collusion
- Any owner who suspects collusion may flag it to the commissioner or in league chat
- Commissioner reserves the right to pause or reverse transactions under collusion review
- If collusion is confirmed, both teams are removed from playoff contention

### Tanking
- Intentionally benching obvious starters to improve draft position is prohibited
- Trading away players to rebuild is not tanking — that is dynasty strategy
- One warning issued before further action is taken

### Roster Dumping
- Dropping obviously valuable players without good reason is subject to commissioner sanctions
- Dropping borderline players is acceptable
- Dumping clear starters or large amounts of players to tank, collude, or sabotage will not be tolerated

- A follow-up vote determines when approved changes take effect
